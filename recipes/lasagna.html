A classic and easy Lasagna


Home

Description

This the classic Italian dish that eveeryone makes in the middle of the week. A hearty and delicious pasta with sauce, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 (32 oz)jar of spaghetti sauce
  • 32 oz cottage cheese
  • 3 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 3/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 T Italian seasoning
  • 3 TB dried parsley
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 10 lasagna noodles
  • 1/2 cup water

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C)
  2. Cook the ground beef in a large pan till the meat is browned. Add in the spaghetti sauce and italian seasoning.
  3. In a bowl combine the cottage cheese, half of the mozzarella, egg, a quarter of the parmesan, parsley, salt, and pepper.
  4. In a 9x13 baking dish, layer the noodles, cheese mixture and meat sauce.
  5. Make sure to sprinkle in the remaining cheeses in between the layers of sauce.
  6. Pour in the water and cover with aluminum foil.
  7. Bake for 45 minutes, then remove the foil and bake for an additional 15 minutes.
  8. Let it sit for 10 minutes then enjoy!
