Description

This recipe has the following fruits & vegetables: eggplant, zucchini, tomatoes, onions, garlic, bell peppers and squash.


This French dish brings out the home cooked flavors of a beautiful french countryside. You can cut the vegetables into bite-sized pieces or slices to help keep them nice and tender.

Ingredients


  • 6 oz tomato paste
  • 1/4 cup minced garlic
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 4 TB olive oil
  • 3 TB mascarpone cheese
  • 1 Small Eggplant
  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 yellow squash
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • yellow bell pepper
  • 1 T of fresh thyme
  • 1/2 onion
