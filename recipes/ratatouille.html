A famous Rat's Ratatouille


Home

Description

This recipe has the following fruits & vegetables: eggplant, zucchini, tomatoes, onions, garlic, bell peppers and squash.

This French dish brings out the home cooked flavors of a beautiful french countryside.

You can cut the vegetables into bite-sized pieces or slices to help keep them nice and tender.

Ingredients

  • 6 oz tomato paste
  • 1/4 cup minced garlic
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 4 TB olive oil
  • 3 TB mascarpone cheese
  • 1 Small Eggplant
  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 yellow squash
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • yellow bell pepper
  • 1 T of fresh thyme
  • 1/2 onion

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Spread the tomato paste to the bottom of a 10 inch dish. Toss in the minced garlic, cut onion, water and a TB of oil.
  3. Slice the eggplant, zucchini, peppers, and squash.alternate the slices around the pan.
  4. Drizzle the vegetables with olive oil and season with salt, pepper, and thyme leaves. then cover with parchment paper.
  5. Bake for 30-40 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender and lightly browned.
  6. After you have let the ratatouille cool for a bit, add a bit of the mascarpone cheese to the top and serve.
